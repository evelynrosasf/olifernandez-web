About Olivier

Pastry chef · Bean-to-bar chocolate maker

I'm Olivier Fernández, a pastry chef and Bean to Bar chocolate maker with over 35 years of experience and a passion for understanding and perfecting every step of the chocolate-making process.

01 · The craft

Over the years, I've dedicated myself to mastering the art of turning cocoa beans into exceptional chocolate, always looking for ways to make the process more efficient without compromising the craftsmanship, quality, and character that make Bean to Bar so special.

02 · The process

Having worked alongside chocolate makers and businesses on projects around the world, I've learned that producing great chocolate isn't just about having the right recipe. It's about understanding the entire process, choosing the right equipment, building a skilled team, and creating a well-structured operation where every step works together.

03 · Today

Today, I use everything I've learned to help others find a simpler, smarter path to producing outstanding chocolate, whether they're starting from scratch or looking to take their production to the next level.

Because I believe that exceptional chocolate shouldn't require an unnecessarily complicated process. It requires knowledge, the right decisions, and a genuine respect for the craft.

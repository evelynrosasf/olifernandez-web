Work

Between the bean and the bar.

My work takes me around the world, collaborating with cocoa producers, chocolate makers, brands, and hospitality teams at different stages of their projects. From assessing existing processes and developing chocolate recipes to selecting equipment, structuring production, scaling operations, and training teams, I focus on finding practical solutions that balance quality, craftsmanship, and efficiency.

I also share my experience through hands-on courses, workshops, conferences, and live demonstrations, connecting with professionals who share my passion for cocoa and chocolate.
